# MDB301: A pinned dataset is retracted

Alert kind `dataset_retracted`, family datasets and papers.

Related watch kinds: `upstream_notice`

## What raises it

The registration agency's record of a pinned dataset DOI says the dataset is retracted. Severity HIGH by policy.

## What it does not prove

It reports the record's state, not why the dataset was retracted. A notice that is not a retraction is an `upstream_notice` watch event.

## How it closes

It is cleared when the record no longer says so, and fixed when a new version no longer pins the dataset.

## How to fix or accept it

Move to another dataset or version and register the new inventory, or dismiss it with a reason. A dismissal holds: a later check that finds the same cause with no new evidence opens no new alert and lists the dismissed alert under the model's `dismissals_held`. New evidence (a changed record, another matched identifier, a higher severity) opens a new alert whose cause names the dismissed one under `reopens`.
