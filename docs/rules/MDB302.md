# MDB302: A paper a pinned dataset cites is retracted

Alert kind `paper_retracted`, family datasets and papers.

Related watch kinds: `paper_not_found`

## What raises it

A paper that a pinned dataset cites is retracted, according to the paper's registration record. Severity HIGH by policy.

## What it does not prove

It does not say the dataset itself is wrong. A paper DOI that cannot be found is a `paper_not_found` watch event.

## How it closes

It is cleared when the paper's record no longer says so, and fixed when a new version no longer pins the dataset.

## How to fix or accept it

Review whether the retraction affects the data you use; move to another dataset, or dismiss it with a reason. A dismissal holds: a later check that finds the same cause with no new evidence opens no new alert and lists the dismissed alert under the model's `dismissals_held`. New evidence (a changed record, another matched identifier, a higher severity) opens a new alert whose cause names the dismissed one under `reopens`.
