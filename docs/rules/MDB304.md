# MDB304: A pinned dataset's DOI is no longer findable

Alert kind `dataset_not_findable`, family datasets and papers.

Related watch kinds: `dataset_unreachable`, `dataset_new_version`

## What raises it

A pinned dataset's DOI is no longer findable at its registration agency. Severity HIGH by policy.

## What it does not prove

It does not prove the data has gone: a repository that does not answer is a `dataset_unreachable` watch event, and a newer version of the dataset is `dataset_new_version`.

## How it closes

It is cleared when the DOI is findable again, and fixed when a new version no longer pins it.

## How to fix or accept it

Check the DOI with its agency and pin a findable identifier, or dismiss it with a reason. A dismissal holds: a later check that finds the same cause with no new evidence opens no new alert and lists the dismissed alert under the model's `dismissals_held`. New evidence (a changed record, another matched identifier, a higher severity) opens a new alert whose cause names the dismissed one under `reopens`.
