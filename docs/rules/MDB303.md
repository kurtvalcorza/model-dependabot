# MDB303: A pinned dataset is removed

Alert kind `dataset_removed`, family datasets and papers.

Related watch kinds: `dataset_moved`, `dataset_replaced`

## What raises it

A pinned dataset is removed: its repository or record reports it deleted or taken down. Severity HIGH by policy.

## What it does not prove

It does not say why it was removed. A dataset that moved is a `dataset_moved` watch event and one replaced by another record is `dataset_replaced`.

## How it closes

It is cleared when the dataset is reachable again, and fixed when a new version no longer pins it.

## How to fix or accept it

Pin a copy you control or another dataset and register the new inventory, or dismiss it with a reason. A dismissal holds: a later check that finds the same cause with no new evidence opens no new alert and lists the dismissed alert under the model's `dismissals_held`. New evidence (a changed record, another matched identifier, a higher severity) opens a new alert whose cause names the dismissed one under `reopens`.
