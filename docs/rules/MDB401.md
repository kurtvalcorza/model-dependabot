# MDB401: The licence of a pinned dependency changed

Alert kind `licence_changed`, family licences.

Related watch kinds: `licence_unrecognised`, `licence_missing`

## What raises it

The licence a source reports for a pinned dependency (a dataset or the base model) differs from the licence the inventory recorded. Severity HIGH by policy.

## What it does not prove

It does not judge whether the new licence allows your use. A licence that cannot be recognised is a `licence_unrecognised` watch event, and one the source does not give is `licence_missing`.

## How it closes

It is cleared when the reported licence matches the recorded one again, and fixed when a new version records the new licence.

## How to fix or accept it

Review the new licence; register a new version that records it, or move to another dependency. A dismissal holds: a later check that finds the same cause with no new evidence opens no new alert and lists the dismissed alert under the model's `dismissals_held`. New evidence (a changed record, another matched identifier, a higher severity) opens a new alert whose cause names the dismissed one under `reopens`.
